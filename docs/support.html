How to play

  1. Add the players and pick how many questions to play.
  2. Read the question out loud and argue about the answer until the timer runs out.
  3. Pass the phone around so everyone can pick A or B.
  4. Reveal the real answer and the fun fact behind it. Right answers score 100 points, plus a 50-point underdog bonus if most of the group was wrong.
