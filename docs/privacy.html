Technically! Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The short version: Technically! doesn't collect, share, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the player names you enter, your game settings, and which questions you've already played, so they're ready for your next game. This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it's deleted when you delete the app.

App updates

The app may download small content and bug-fix updates from Expo, the service used to build it. Like any internet request, these update checks include basic technical information such as your IP address, device platform, and app version. It's used only to deliver the right update and isn't used to identify or track you.

Children

Technically! is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
